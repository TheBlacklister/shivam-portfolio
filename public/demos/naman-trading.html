Questions we get asked

When is my atta milled?

We mill in small batches. Every pack carries its milling date, and most orders leave within a day or two of grinding.

Do you deliver outside Delhi NCR?

Not yet. We deliver to Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram and Faridabad. Enter your pincode above to check.

Is there a minimum order?

No. Delivery is free on orders of ₹599 or more, and ₹40 below that.

How can I pay?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery for orders up to ₹5,000.

Can I return an order?

If a pack arrives damaged, torn or is the wrong item, tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we will replace it or refund you. Opened food packs cannot be returned for other reasons.

How should I store fresh chakki atta?

Keep it in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Millet flours like bajra are best used within a month; refrigerate them in summer.

Is multigrain atta gluten-free?

No, it contains wheat. For gluten-free cooking choose jowar, bajra, ragi, rice, besan, kuttu or singhara flour, or our gluten-free atta.
