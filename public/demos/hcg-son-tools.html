Help

How do I know which part fits my machine?

Search by the model number printed on your machine's label, or check the "Fits" line on each part. If you are unsure, WhatsApp us a photo of the machine label or the old part and we will confirm the match before you order.

What if the part does not fit?

Unused parts in their original packing can be exchanged or refunded within 7 days. Electrical parts such as armatures, field coils and switches cannot be returned once they have been fitted or powered.

Is there a warranty?

Machines carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Spare parts are checked before dispatch; tell us within 48 hours if anything arrives faulty.

Do you deliver outside Kolkata?

Yes, by courier across India in 3 to 5 working days. Enter your pincode above to check.

How can I pay?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery for orders up to ₹10,000 in Kolkata and nearby districts.
